# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Police supervisory officers at multiple command levels (ASP/DySP, Inspector, Sub Inspector), SCRB analysts, and policymakers. Their context is operational — station desks, control rooms, and command centers — where they analyze crime data, review FIRs, optimize beat allocations, track officer performance, and generate intelligence reports under time pressure.

## Product Purpose

AI-driven crime analytics and visualization platform for the Karnataka State Police. Transforms siloed, manual crime records into an integrated intelligence hub with network analysis, predictive risk scoring, spatiotemporal hotspot detection, and conversational AI querying. Moves policing from reactive to proactive through data-driven decision support.

## Brand Personality

Authoritative, precise, commanding. The interface projects the confidence and gravity of a law enforcement command center. Every element communicates reliability and exactness. No frivolity, no ornament — only operational purpose.

## Anti-references

- Government portal aesthetic (cluttered, dated, low-contrast form-heavy layouts)
- Generic corporate SaaS dashboards (soft gradients, rounded everything, "friendly" data viz)
- Flashy consumer app design (neon gradients, bouncy animations, gamification)
- Traditional govt IT looks (blue gradients, heavy shadows, glossy buttons, clip-art icons)
- Dark CRT terminal aesthetics (zero radius, monospace body, red accent, harsh contrast)

## Design Principles

1. **Command at a glance** — Critical information must be readable within seconds. High data density with ruthless visual hierarchy.
2. **Every pixel serves a purpose** — No decorative elements without operational meaning. The interface is an instrument, not a canvas.
3. **Authority through precision** — Layout, typography, and spacing communicate exactness. Nothing feels approximate or indulgent.
4. **Data leads** — Visualizations, networks, and telemetry drive the interface. Chrome is minimal; content dominates.
5. **Respect the operator** — Designed for high-stakes environments where clarity under pressure matters. Zero cognitive overhead from unnecessary embellishment.
6. **Premium utilitarian** — Borrow Apple's approach to complex data: warm monochrome, generous whitespace, system fonts, restrained accent color. Authority through calm, not intensity.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.1 AA compliance. Considerations for color vision deficiency (rely on indicators beyond color alone), reduced motion preferences, and keyboard navigation for power users.

## Design System

Apple Premium Utilitarian Minimalism. Light theme with warm bone (#f5f5f7) background, pure white (#ffffff) surfaces, Apple Blue (#0071e3) accent, and system font stack (SF Pro). See DESIGN.md for full specification.
